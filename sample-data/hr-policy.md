# HR Policy

Document classification: Internal  
Source department: Human Resources  
Access group: all-employees  
Version: 1.0

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are based on local employment contracts and applicable labor law. Managers may approve flexible schedules when business coverage is maintained.

## Leave Requests

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the company HR system at least ten business days in advance where feasible. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as practical.

## Salary Data

Individual salary data is restricted and must not be shared outside authorized HR and payroll processes. Aggregated compensation reporting must be anonymized and approved by HR leadership.

## Conduct

Employees are expected to follow the code of conduct, protect confidential information, and report concerns through approved reporting channels.
