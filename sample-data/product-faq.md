# Product FAQ

Document classification: Public  
Source department: Product  
Access group: all-employees  
Version: 1.0

## What does the Enterprise RAG Governance Platform do?

It allows employees to ask questions over approved internal documents and receive answers grounded in retrieved source context.

## Why are citations important?

Citations help users verify whether an answer is supported by company documents. They also help reviewers inspect the source context used by the model.

## Can the platform answer from restricted documents?

Only authorized users should retrieve restricted documents. Retrieval filters must be enforced by the backend before answer generation.

## What happens when context is insufficient?

The assistant should say that it does not know rather than inventing unsupported information.
