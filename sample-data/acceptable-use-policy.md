# Acceptable Use Policy

Document classification: Internal  
Source department: Information Security  
Access group: all-employees  
Version: 1.0

## System Use

Company systems must be used for authorized business purposes. Incidental personal use must not interfere with work, security, or legal obligations.

## Prohibited Activities

Users must not attempt to bypass access controls, scan systems without approval, install unapproved software, or exfiltrate company data.

## AI Tools

Employees must not paste confidential or restricted company information into unapproved AI tools. Approved AI systems must follow access control, logging, and data protection requirements.

## Reporting

Suspected misuse, phishing, malware, or accidental disclosure must be reported to IT Security promptly.
