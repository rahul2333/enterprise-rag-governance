# Data Protection Policy

Document classification: Confidential  
Source department: Legal and Privacy  
Access group: privacy-team  
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This policy defines how synthetic company data should be classified, retained, and protected in internal systems.

## Data Minimization

Teams must collect only the information needed for a documented business purpose. Data fields that are not required should be removed before processing.

## Retention

Documents containing confidential business information must have an assigned retention period. Expired records should be deleted or archived according to the retention schedule.

## Data Subject Requests

Requests related to personal data must be routed to the Privacy team. Demo environments must use synthetic data only and must not include real personal data.

## Third-Party Processing

Vendors that process confidential data must have approved contractual controls, security review, and documented processing purpose.
