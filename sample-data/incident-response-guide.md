# Incident Response Guide

Document classification: Confidential  
Source department: Security Operations  
Access group: security-team  
Version: 1.0

## Incident Severity

Incidents are categorized as low, medium, high, or critical based on business impact, data sensitivity, and service availability.

## Initial Response

The first responder should record the time detected, affected system, suspected scope, and immediate containment actions.

## Communication

Security Operations coordinates incident communication with Legal, Privacy, Communications, and affected business owners. External notifications require approval from Legal and executive leadership.

## Evidence Handling

Logs, alerts, and forensic artifacts must be preserved in approved evidence storage. Evidence must not be copied into personal drives or unmanaged tools.

## Lessons Learned

A post-incident review should be completed for high and critical incidents within ten business days.
