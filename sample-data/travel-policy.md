# Travel Policy

Document classification: Internal  
Source department: Finance  
Access group: all-employees  
Version: 1.0

## Booking

Business travel should be booked through the approved travel portal. Travelers should select reasonable fares and comply with budget guidance.

## Expenses

Expense claims must include a business purpose, receipt when required, and accurate cost center. Claims should be submitted within thirty days of travel completion.

## Personal Travel

Personal travel may not be charged to the company. Combining business and personal travel requires manager approval and clear separation of costs.

## Data Handling During Travel

Travelers should avoid accessing confidential documents on public devices or unsecured networks.
